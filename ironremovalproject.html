Iron Removal Plants for Tripura Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM)


Technochem is proud to be a key partner in the Tripura government's Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) initiative. This flagship program aims to ensure sustainable and clean drinking water for rural households across the state. Recognizing the significant challenge posed by iron contamination in groundwater, Technochem has taken up the task of designing and constructing Iron Removal Plants (IRPs) tailored to the needs of various regions in Tripura.

Project Scope

Technochem has successfully designed and implemented multiple IRPs under the JJM scheme, with varying capacities to address the diverse requirements of the local population. These plants ensure that treated water meets drinking water quality standards, free from excessive iron and other impurities.

Key Features of the Plants

  • Capacities:
    • 2500 Gallons Per Hour (GPH): Suitable for smaller communities.
    • 5000 Gallons Per Hour (GPH): Ideal for medium-sized villages and institutions.
    • 10,000 Gallons Per Hour (GPH): Designed for larger settlements and high-demand areas.
  • Advanced Technology: Effective removal of iron and other impurities using a combination of filtration and oxidation processes.
  • Sustainability: Energy-efficient solutions tailored to the power availability in rural Tripura.
  • Compliance: Adheres to Indian drinking water quality standards.

Impact

Technochem's Iron Removal Plants have significantly improved the quality of drinking water for communities in Tripura, fostering better health outcomes and reducing the risks associated with iron-contaminated water. These projects not only align with the vision of Jal Jeevan Mission but also underscore Technochem's commitment to sustainable and impactful water treatment solutions.

Ongoing Work

Technochem continues to collaborate with the Tripura government on upcoming JJM projects, expanding the reach of clean water solutions to even more households. Our dedicated team is focused on innovation, community engagement, and delivering reliable infrastructure for a brighter future.
