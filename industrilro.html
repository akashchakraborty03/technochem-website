Scope

What Is a Custom-Designed RO System — and Who Needs One?

A reverse osmosis (RO) system forces water through a semi-permeable membrane to strip out dissolved salts, hardness, heavy metals, and microbial contaminants. A standard RO unit is built around assumed, average water conditions. A custom RO system manufacturer instead starts with your actual feed water report — TDS, hardness, iron, pH, microbial load — and your required flow rate, then designs pre-treatment and membrane staging specifically for that combination.

Benefits

Benefits of a Custom-Designed RO System

When the system is engineered around your actual water and output target, the gains show up in day-to-day operations, not just on a datasheet:

Lower operating cost

Pre-treatment matched to your feed water means less membrane fouling and fewer cleaning-in-place cycles.

Consistent permeate quality

Hits your target TDS and hardness reliably, which matters when pharma or food-grade compliance is on the line.

Higher recovery, less reject water

More usable water from the same source, especially valuable where water availability is tight.

Less unplanned downtime

Pumps and membranes sized correctly aren't overworked or underworked.

Easier regulatory compliance

Output engineered to meet IS 10500, CPCB, or sector-specific norms.

Built-in scalability

Modular design lets you add capacity later without re-engineering the whole plant.

Better energy efficiency

Right-sized high-pressure pumps reduce power consumption per litre produced.

Case Studies

Representative Projects & Results

The scenarios below reflect the type of work we regularly execute for clients in these sectors. Specific outcomes vary by site, feed water quality, and configuration.

Packaged Drinking Water Unit — West Bengal

Challenge: Borewell feed water at roughly 1,800 ppm TDS, with a requirement for 2,000 LPH output suitable for packaged drinking water standards and minimal reject loss.

Solution: Multi-stage sand and activated carbon pre-treatment, dual 8040 membrane configuration, dosing tuned to the feed chemistry, and an SS structure with a 2,000-litre SS storage tank.

Result: A system engineered to handle the higher TDS load while controlling recovery loss, with monitoring needs reduced from daily to weekly checks once the dosing was stabilized.

Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Unit — Eastern India

Challenge: RO-grade input water required ahead of an injectable-grade purification stage, with fluctuating municipal hardness levels.

Solution: Water softening paired with an RO skid and full instrumentation (rotameters, pressure gauges) fitted into an existing utility room footprint.

Result: Stable, consistent feed quality into the next purification stage, with a lower membrane replacement frequency thanks to correctly sized pre-treatment.

Boutique Hotel — Kolkata

Challenge: A 1,000 LPH RO plant required in a tight utility space for guest drinking water and laundry use, against an intermittent municipal supply.

Solution: A compact SS-skid 1,000 LPH RO unit with a buffer tank and automated low/high-pressure switches to manage supply interruptions.

Result: Uninterrupted clean water supply to guest floors and the laundry, with the plant fitting entirely within the available utility space.
