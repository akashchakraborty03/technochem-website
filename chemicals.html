Turnkey Wastewater Management Company for Industries

Untreated or poorly treated effluent is one of the fastest ways for an industrial unit, hotel, hospital, or residential township to end up in front of a pollution control board. The usual workaround — hiring a design consultant, an equipment fabricator, a civil contractor, and an operations crew separately — turns a compliance requirement into a year-long coordination problem, with no single team accountable for the finished plant.

This is exactly the gap Technochem closes. As a turnkey wastewater management company based in Kolkata, with our own design office and manufacturing facility, we plan, engineer, fabricate, install, and operate complete effluent treatment plant (ETP) and sewage treatment plant (STP) systems under one contract, one timeline, and one point of accountability — from the first site visit to the day your plant is signed off by regulators.


Scope

What Is Turnkey Wastewater Management — And Who It's For

A turnkey project means exactly what it sounds like: you get a working plant, not a stack of drawings and a list of vendors left to chase down. Our end-to-end wastewater solutions cover the full lifecycle of a project:

Full Lifecycle Coverage

  • Effluent and sewage characterisation and load calculation
  • Process design and statutory drawing approval
  • Equipment manufacturing at our own ISO 9001:2015 certified facility
  • Civil construction, piping, electricals, and instrumentation
  • Installation, commissioning, and trial runs
  • Liaison with pollution control boards and other statutory bodies
  • Ongoing operation, maintenance, and AMC support after handover

Who This Model Is Built For

This model works best for organisations that generate effluent or sewage as part of daily operations and need it handled correctly, without building an in-house engineering team to manage it:

  • Manufacturing Units — textile and dyeing, pharmaceutical, chemical, food & beverage, leather and tanning, and engineering or metal-finishing plants
  • Hospitality and Healthcare — hotels, resorts, and hospitals managing sewage plus high-BOD kitchen or laundry effluent
  • Institutions and campuses — colleges, universities, and corporate parks with on-site sewage treatment obligations
  • Real estate and townships — builders, RWAs, and housing societies needing STPs for occupancy certificates and water reuse
  • Municipal and government bodies — civic and public-sector projects that need large-scale, compliant sewage infrastructure

If your facility falls into any of these categories, industrial wastewater management isn't optional — it's a licence-to-operate issue, and it's exactly where a dedicated, single-vendor partner saves the most time and money.

Process

Our Process: From Site Visit to Commissioned Plant

1
Site Assessment & Effluent Characterisation

We visit your site, measure flow rates, and test effluent or sewage characteristics (BOD, COD, TSS, pH, and any sector-specific parameters) to understand exactly what needs to be treated.

2
Custom Engineering & Design

Our engineering team designs a plant sized to your actual load, available footprint, and discharge or reuse goals, then prepares drawings for your approval and for statutory submission.

3
Manufacturing & Quality Testing

Core equipment is fabricated at our ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturing facility on NH-34, Khilkapur, under in-house quality control, rather than outsourced to third-party job-work shops.

4
Civil & Mechanical Installation

Our project team handles civil construction, piping, tankage, electricals, and instrumentation, coordinating directly with your site team to minimise disruption to ongoing operations.

5
Commissioning & Trial Runs

The plant is started up, calibrated, and run through trial cycles until treated output consistently meets the design parameters before we call it complete.

6
Statutory Liaison & Documentation

We assist with the compliance documentation and pollution control board liaison needed to get your plant — and your facility — formally signed off.

7
Operation, Maintenance & AMC

Post-handover, our O&M and annual maintenance contract (AMC) packages keep the plant running at design performance, with scheduled servicing and rapid response if something needs attention.
