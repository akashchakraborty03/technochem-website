Frequently Asked Questions

What types of water treatment solutions do you provide?
At Technochem, we specialize in a wide range of water treatment solutions including sewage treatment plants (STP), iron removal plants, reverse osmosis (RO) systems, arsenic removal, and customized water treatment systems for various needs.
How can I choose the right water treatment system for my needs?
We conduct a thorough analysis of your water quality and specific requirements to recommend the best solution, considering water source, contamination levels, desired quality, and budget.
What industries do you serve?
We serve a diverse range of industries including residential, commercial, healthcare, education, hospitality, and industrial sectors.
Do you offer maintenance services for water treatment systems?
Yes, we provide comprehensive maintenance and after-sales support to ensure your system operates efficiently and has a long lifespan. Our team conducts regular checkups, filter replacements, and system servicing as needed.
How can I get a quote for my project?
You can contact us via our website, phone, or email to discuss your project requirements. We will conduct an initial assessment and provide a detailed quotation based on your specific needs.
What should I do if I encounter an issue with my system?
In case of any issues, you can reach out to our support team. Our technicians are ready to assist with troubleshooting, repairs, and servicing to ensure your system functions optimally.
Can I track my order or project progress?
Yes, our team provides regular updates on your project progress. For larger projects, we assign a dedicated project manager who will keep you informed at every stage of development, from design to installation.
